# S10-pay-razorpay

## Summary

DECISION (unchanged): S10 stops being an Ohm screen. Pay on S06 or S13 opens Razorpay straight away. Node 9:7787 keeps its id and is rebuilt in place as 'S10 Razorpay netbanking', the first Razorpay frame. Ohm keeps only what Razorpay cannot say: the Pay button with today's amount, the trust line, the 'Confirming' and 'Waiting for your bank' pages, and the success, failure and cancel states back on S06.\n\nWHY NO CONFIRM STEP: the two choices S10 asked for are gone (one amount is valid today, the method is Razorpay's job), the button already carries the amount, and two more gates follow before money moves (Razorpay's Pay Now, then her bank's login and approval). A confirm screen would be a third confirmation of one action. Taps inside Ohm to reach Razorpay go from 2 to 1.\n\nWHAT CHANGED IN THIS REVISION (critique answered, nothing high or medium left open):\n1. PENDING STATE ADDED (high). S10.3 no longer promises 'a few seconds'. After about 20 s without a result, or at once if the server reports the payment as pending, she sees new S10.5 'Waiting for your bank' with 'You can leave this page. We will tell you when it is confirmed.' and a 'Back to bill' button. Home gets a Layout=Pending card, S06 gets a Pending footer (S06.3), and three notification texts are written. The 1,200 ms jump from S10.3 to S11 is labelled a demo shortcut.\n2. DOUBLE-PAYMENT GUARD ADDED (high). One open order per bill and amount, reused on every tap. If any payment on the bill is created, authorized or captured, the server refuses a new order and S06 swaps the Pay pill for a status footer ('Payment pending' or 'Payment sent'). The result route uses replace, so Back never returns to a live Pay button. The footer is now one component, 'Ohm / Pay footer', State=Pay, Pending or Sent.\n3. ONE AMOUNT, REALLY (high). On S06 the hero is relabelled 'Bill total · due Wed 16 Sep' so it reads as Northgrid's number, and the discount card no longer repeats ₹36,09,190: it says 'Pay by Tue 8 Sep and save ₹32,971'. Only the Pay button and Razorpay carry the amount payable today. No subtraction is ever shown (₹36,42,160 minus ₹32,971 gives ₹36,09,189, the bill prints ₹36,09,190).\n4. LOGO MADE A GATE (high). I cannot download assets without the lead's explicit yes, so the build order puts logo approval first (see open questions 2). Until then every logo slot shows a visibly marked placeholder with a review note, not a silent grey box. Bank marks follow the same rule; without them the bank grid is text-only tiles, never six identical landmark icons.\n5. METHODS LIST OFF THE MAIN PATH (medium). Razorpay's docs confirm prefill.method (netbanking) for Standard Checkout, so the happy path opens on the bank picker. The old 'Payment Options' list is kept as one reference frame (S10.7), with the dead Bank Transfer row removed. Whether it really is skipped must be confirmed in Razorpay test mode.\n6. FAILURE MESSAGE NO LONGER TWICE (medium). S10.4 keeps Razorpay's wording. S06.1 shrinks to 'Payment did not go through' plus 'Nothing was paid to Northgrid.' (one line, alert 76 tall, S06.1 = S06 + 88). It also has to stand alone because a redirect failure can land on S06 without S10.4.\n7. SMALLER EDGE CASES (medium). Overdue alert reduced to 'Overdue' + 'Northgrid now charges ₹45,530 more.'. The Amount changed drawer and the Status=Clock-for-S10.5 idea are cut: on a stale page S06 refreshes in place. One new illustration variant is added only for the pending page (see illustrations, with the reason).\n8. TOASTS UNIFIED (medium). The existing 'Toast' component is reused (361 x 48, 12 above the footer, one line, 42 characters or fewer, 4 s) with a Tone property. Errors are inline Alerts on S06, not toasts.\n9. HEADER FIXED (medium). The merchant tile is an instance of 'Ohm / App icon', and the payee 'August bill, Northgrid Electric' is drawn in the header. Chip contrast is now 6.5 to 1, all header targets are 44 x 44, and 'View Details' has a 44 high hit area.\n10. FOOTER SLIMMED (medium). The pinned footer is Pay plus trust line only (128 tall). 'Share with owner' moves into the scrolling content after the breakdown card. No secondary action sits next to Pay.\n11. WEB-BUILDABLE (medium). Haptics removed everywhere. S10.3, S10.5 and S11 are one route with in-page states, so the Hero to Compact shrink is real even after a bank redirect.\n12. LOW ITEMS DONE: S10 has no bank selected and Pay Now disabled, S10.1 has HDFC selected; the failure path is now a visible button on a demo frame, not a hidden key; Scan=None on the confirming bill; 'You saved' replaced by a rule that does not claim the saving before Northgrid confirms; notification copy added.\n\nSTILL BLOCKING (open questions 1): Razorpay's published default netbanking limit is ₹5,00,000. This bill is about 7 times that. The prototype draws netbanking and assumes the limit is raised.

## Illustrations

### Bill, confirming (S10.3)
- Concept: The approved S04 object doing a related job: the white bill page of skeleton lines with a round corner badge holding a spinner. The scan line is left out on purpose: in Ohm the line means 'reading your bill', so reusing it here could read as Ohm re-reading the bill. The picture only says 'one moment', and the words say what for.
- Construction: No new art. Instance of 'Illustration / Bill', Size=Hero (240 x 290), Focus=None, Status=Reading, Scan=None (line and glow at 0% opacity). The round 1 variant list has Hero/None/Reading only for the four scan values, so build this one by duplicating Hero/None/Reading/Checks and setting line and glow to 0%, which is the construction the spec already defines for Scan=None. Badge 52 x 52, white, 1 px line stroke, kit Spinner 24 in primary. Layer names identical so Smart animate to S11 works.
- Hover: No hover on touch. Only the spinner turns. On the move to S11 (same route, in-page state) the hero shrinks to Compact over 300 ms ease out while the spinner crossfades to the check over 200 ms, the icon scaling 0.6 to 1 with a soft spring. Reduce Motion: crossfade only.
### Bill, waiting for your bank (S10.5)
- Concept: The same bill, now with a warning clock badge: time is passing on someone else's side (the bank), nothing is wrong with the bill or with Ohm. This is the foundation rule R3 (waiting on someone else is warning plus clock, the same pair as 'Paid, waiting for Northgrid to confirm' on S11). It is also why Status=Reading would be wrong here: a spinner says Ohm is working.
- Construction: Add ONE variant to the Status property of 'Illustration / Bill': Status=Clock, used only as Hero / Focus=None / Scan=None. Badge 52 x 52 circle, fill brand/warning-subtle, no stroke, Lucide clock 24 stroke 2 in brand/warning, same position as the other badges. No other variants and no Compact version. This replaces the cut 'amount changed' badge idea; it is justified by a core state, not a midnight edge case.
- Hover: The clock's minute hand turns slowly, one full turn every 8 s, linear, and keeps going while the page is open. Reduce Motion: static at 10 past.
### Bill, paid (S11, replaces the plain green circle)
- Concept: The same bill page, now stamped with the green round badge, so Paid is the end of the story S04 started. It also matches the small bill with a tick on the Home card.
- Construction: No new art. Instance of 'Illustration / Bill', Size=Compact (240 x 128), Focus=Maths, Status=Done (success badge, no scan line), centred, top at y 86.
